# Empório DaTerra — Guaratiba, Prado, Bahia

Independent shareable website preview for the restaurant and accommodation.
- Real brand logotype and restaurant content supplied by the owner (screenshots of @emporiodaterraguaratiba).

- The preview is noindex, nofollow until the final domain is connected.
- All external booking and restaurant contact buttons point to Airbnb and Instagram.
